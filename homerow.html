    • F and J have raised bumps — use them to find home position without looking down.
    • Curve your fingers lightly — rest them gently on the keys, not pressing down.
    • Return after every keystroke — always bring fingers back to home row after reaching for another key.
    • Eyes on the screen — the keyboard diagram at the bottom of each session shows which finger to use.
