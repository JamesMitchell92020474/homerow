HomeRow — Sound Generator

This one-time utility generates the WAV audio files needed by the HomeRow typing tutor. Run it once, download both files, and place them in assets/sounds/.

keypress.wav

Soft click played on each correct keystroke

error.wav

Short buzz played on each incorrect keystroke

How to use this tool:
1. Click "Generate & Download Both Sound Files" above.
2. Save keypress.wav and error.wav into the assets/sounds/ folder of your HomeRow project.
3. That's it — you won't need this tool again unless you want different sounds.

Note: Your browser may download both files simultaneously or prompt you to allow multiple downloads.
